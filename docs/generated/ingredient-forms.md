# Ingredient forms

Generated from `data/ingredients.csv`. Bioavailability ratings summarise human data; they are not percentages unless stated. `Evidence quality` refers to confidence in the comparison, not in a health benefit.

## Vitamin A

| Form | Class | Bioavailability | Adult UL | Typical multi dose | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retinol / retinyl palmitate / retinyl acetate | Preformed | High | 3000 mcg RAE | 400-1500 mcg RAE | The form that carries the teratogenicity and hepatotoxicity risk. A multivitamin should keep preformed retinol moderate. |
| beta-Carotene | Provitamin A | Low-moderate | none established | 0.5-3 mg | No UL, but 20-30 mg/day increased lung cancer risk in smokers. A 'vegetarian' multi may use it as its only vitamin A, which makes the label amount misleading. |
| Mixed carotenoids | Provitamin A | Low-moderate | none established | 0.3-3 mg | Includes lutein, lycopene and alpha-carotene. Useful for eye health at AREDS2 doses, not a substitute for retinol in deficiency. |
